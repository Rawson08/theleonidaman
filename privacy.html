Privacy Policy

TheLeonidaMan — last updated July 20, 2026

TheLeonidaMan ("the App") is a personal, single-user content-publishing tool. This policy describes what data the App handles.

1. What data the App accesses

Through the TikTok API, and only after the account owner grants authorization, the App accesses: basic profile information of the owner's own TikTok account (display name, avatar) and the ability to upload videos to that account. The App does not access followers, messages, comments, or any data belonging to other TikTok users.

2. What data the App stores

OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own accounts are stored locally on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is stored on any server, and nothing is transmitted anywhere except directly to the platform APIs (TikTok, Meta) over HTTPS.

3. What the App does NOT do

  • No collection of personal data from any third party
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No processing of children's data

4. Data deletion

Revoking the App's access in TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps) immediately invalidates its tokens. Locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time by deleting the token file.
